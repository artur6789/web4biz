Privacy policy

Data controller

Artur Załuska operates web4biz.pl and is responsible for your personal data. For privacy matters, email kontakt@web4biz.pl.

What I process and why

When you send an enquiry, I process your name, email address, selected service tier and message. I use this information to respond and, where appropriate, prepare a quote. Providing it is voluntary. I need your email address and message to reply.

If you request a quote on your own behalf before entering a contract, the basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For other enquiries, including enquiries made on behalf of a business, the basis is my legitimate interest in responding to correspondence under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Who receives your data

Form submissions are delivered through FormSubmit.co (Devro LABS) to the contact mailbox. The website hosting and email providers also handle data in providing their services.

How long I keep it

I retain correspondence as long as needed to respond and conclude project discussions. If a contract or claim arises, I may keep the necessary data longer to perform the contract, meet legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. FormSubmit states in its documentation that it keeps form submissions for 30 days.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure or restriction. Where applicable under the GDPR, you also have the right to data portability and to object to processing based on a legitimate interest. Contact me at the address above to exercise these rights. You can also complain to the Polish Personal Data Protection Office.

Site preferences

The site stores theme and language preferences in your browser. You can clear them in your browser settings.
